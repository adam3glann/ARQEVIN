# Client discovery questionnaire

Use this as a conversation guide. Ask only what is relevant; do not request passwords, full payment details, or sensitive personal information in this document.

## Business and goal

1. What does the business do, and who are its customers?
2. What are you hoping to improve or build?
3. Why is this important now?
4. How will you know the first release is useful?

## Current process and users

1. How do people handle this today?
2. Where does time, information, or customer interest get lost?
3. Who will use the solution, and what should each user be able to do?
4. Is there a process, policy, or accessibility need the solution must respect?

## Scope and integrations

1. What are the three most important tasks the first release must support?
2. What can be left for a later phase?
3. Does it need to exchange data with an existing system or provider?
4. For commerce, which catalog, order, delivery, and payment workflows are needed?
5. For AI or automation, what inputs and outputs are expected, and where must a person review the result?

## Content, data, and access

1. Who will provide text, images, product details, and brand assets?
2. Who owns the existing data, and what format is it in?
3. What user roles or access boundaries are needed?
4. Are there data handling, retention, backup, or audit requirements?
5. Who can provide test accounts or safe sample data? Never send real passwords through this questionnaire.

## Timing, budget, and decisions

1. Is there a target launch date? What drives it?
2. Is there an approximate budget range for the first release?
3. Who makes scope and payment decisions?
4. Who will review milestones, and how quickly can they usually respond?
5. Are there dependencies such as content, a vendor account, or approval from another team?

## After launch

1. Who will maintain content and user access?
2. What support would be useful after launch?
3. What would you want to improve next if the first release works well?
